Walmart Ecommerce Sales Analysis

Project Overview

This is my first Power BI project. It explores ecommerce sales performance and business insights using an interactive dashboard.

Project Objectives

- Analyze sales amount and quantity.
- Compare sales across states.
- Understand customer payment preferences.
- Track monthly profit trends.
- Analyze product category and sub-category performance.

Tools Used

- Microsoft Power BI
- Data Visualization
- Interactive Filters and Slicers

Dashboard KPIs

- Total Sales Amount
- Total Quantity Sold
- Total Profit
- AOD (Amount divided by Quantity)

Key Insights

- Maharashtra has the highest sales amount among the states shown in the dashboard.
- Cash on Delivery is the largest payment mode by quantity in the displayed dashboard.
- Monthly profit varies, and some months show negative profit.
- Clothing contributes the largest share of quantity sold among the displayed categories.
