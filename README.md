Walmart Ecommerce Sales Analysis Dashboard

  Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze ecommerce sales performance, profitability, customer payment preferences, and product trends. It transforms sales data into meaningful visual insights to support better business understanding and data-driven decision-making.

 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales amount, quantity sold, and profit.
- Compare sales performance across different states.
- Understand customer payment preferences and payment-mode distribution.
- Monitor monthly profit trends and identify loss-making periods.
- Evaluate product category and sub-category performance.
- Identify useful business insights through interactive data visualization.

 Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI — Dashboard development and reporting.
- Data Visualization — Presenting sales and profit trends through charts.
- Interactive Filters & Slicers — Exploring data by state and quarter.
- Business Analysis — Interpreting sales performance and product trends.

 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

- Total Sales Amount: Overall sales value represented in the dashboard.
- Total Quantity Sold: Total quantity of products sold.
- Total Profit: Overall profit represented in the dashboard.
- AOD (Amount per Unit): Amount divided by quantity, as defined in this project.

 Key Business Insights

- State-wise Performance: Maharashtra has the highest sales amount among the states displayed in the dashboard.
- Payment Preferences: Cash on Delivery (COD) accounts for the largest share of quantity among the displayed payment modes.
- Monthly Profitability: Monthly profit varies, with some months showing negative profit and indicating periods worth investigating.
- Category Performance: Clothing contributes the largest share of quantity sold among the displayed product categories.
- Product-level Profitability: Profit differs across sub-categories, helping identify products that may deserve further investigation.

 Business Value

This dashboard helps users explore sales performance, understand customer payment patterns, monitor profitability, and identify product-level trends. These insights can support further investigation into sales opportunities and areas for business improvement.

 Key Learning Outcomes

- Practised building an interactive Power BI dashboard.
- Worked with KPI cards, charts, filters, and slicers.
- Explored sales, quantity, and profit trends.
- Developed a practical understanding of business-focused data visualization.
